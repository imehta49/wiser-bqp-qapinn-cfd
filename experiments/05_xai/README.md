# Section 05 — Explainability and Learning Dynamics

Planned analyses:

1. Fourier spectra of quantum features, classical features, final fields, and residuals.
2. Parameter-gradient norms and variances versus qubit count and circuit depth.
3. Classical and quantum/hybrid neural tangent-kernel spectra.
4. Two-dimensional loss-landscape sections.
5. Entanglement entropy and entangler-removal ablations.
6. Correlation of explainability quantities with accuracy and generalization.

The objective is to explain what is gained and lost by introducing the quantum layer, not simply to rank final errors.
